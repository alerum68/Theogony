# Family group sheets and reports

Theogony provides standard genealogical reports and family group sheets designed for clear documentation, proof packets, and print sharing. Open these reports from the Reports view on the activity bar whenever you need to compile a family record or audit research across places, citations, and media.

## The Family Group Sheet

The **Family Group Sheet** is the fundamental workhorse document of family history research. It summarizes a complete nuclear family unit—the parents and all their children—on a structured, printable page.

### What it shows
- **Husband / Father:** Full name, birth date and place, baptism, death date and place, burial place, and parents' names.
- **Wife / Mother:** Full name (including maiden name), birth, baptism, death, burial, and parents' names.
- **Marriage Event:** Marriage date, official jurisdiction, officiating authority, and marriage bond / license citations.
- **Children List:** All children born to the couple, arranged strictly in chronological birth order, displaying:
  - Child's full name and sex.
  - Birth date and location.
  - Marriage date, place, and spouse's name.
  - Death date and location.
- **Citations and Endnotes:** Complete source citations verifying each date, place, and relationship recorded on the sheet.

### Common tasks
1. In the workbench, select **Add View** $\rightarrow$ **Family Group Sheet** (or select **Reports** on the activity bar and switch to the Family Group Sheet view).
2. The sheet automatically populates with the currently focused family from your active individual.
3. Select **Export PDF...** to generate a clean, publication-ready vector PDF using the standard archival print palette.

## Specialized Research Reports

In addition to Family Group Sheets, Theogony includes specialized audit reports to assist with research analysis:

### 1. Place Reports
- **Purpose:** Groups all life events in your database by geographic jurisdiction.
- **When to use:** When planning a courthouse or library research trip to a specific county (e.g., *Clark County, Ohio*). Open the Place Report to print an inventory of every birth, marriage, deed, census, and death that occurred in that county across your entire tree.

### 2. Citation Reports
- **Purpose:** Lists every citation attached to a specific master source or repository.
- **When to use:** Auditing evidence quality. Open a Citation Report for a specific probate book or census microfilm to verify that all page numbers, volume numbers, and verbatim transcriptions are complete.

### 3. DNA Reports
- **Purpose:** Compiles a structured catalog of all imported genetic kits, match lists, and painted chromosome segments.
- **When to use:** Preparing genetic proof arguments or documenting shared segment triangulations for formal lineage reports.

### 4. Media Reports
- **Purpose:** Audits all digital photographs, scanned certificates, and historical documents in your tree.
- **When to use:** Identifying unlinked media items or verifying that all vital event scans are properly associated with master source records.

## Practical use cases

- **Family reunion binder compilation:** Print out Family Group Sheets for each ancestral branch to assemble a comprehensive, readable family binder for relatives.
- **Courthouse research checklists:** Generate a Place Report for an ancestral county to take a complete list of names and approximate dates to the archives.
- **Lineage society documentation:** Print Family Group Sheets complete with citation endnotes to include in DAR, SAR, or general genealogical society proof packets.

## Good to know

- Family Group Sheets and reports adhere to the fixed **Print Palette** (`Newsreader` serif typography, dark ink rules, and clean tabular alignment) independent of your active screen theme.
- Living individuals on reports are automatically redacted according to privacy policies.
- Reports can be copied directly to the clipboard or exported to vector PDF format.
