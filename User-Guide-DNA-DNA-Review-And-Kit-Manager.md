# DNA review and kit manager

![DNA review and kit manager](images/user-guide/dna-review.png)

Manage imported autosomal, Y-DNA, and mtDNA kits, testing companies, match counts, and segment overlap statistics. Open this screen when you want to review DNA matches, link kits to individuals in your tree, or resolve convergence issues.

## What you see

The screen is divided into sections covering different aspects of your DNA data management. The DNA Kits section lists imported testing companies, kit identifiers, display names, and haplogroups alongside their link status. The Recently auto-merged ancestors section displays names of individuals combined during recent scans. The Match-tree personas to review section provides a table for matching personas to tree individuals. The Possible shared ancestors across matches section handles duplicate candidates and potential shared ancestors.

## Common tasks

### Review match-tree personas

1. Locate the Match-tree personas to review section.
2. Select **Attach…** on a row to open the persona attachment dialog.

### Link a DNA kit

1. Find an unlinked kit in the DNA Kits section.
2. Select **Link to individual** and pick the matching person from the list.

## Good to know

The kit table updates automatically when you run a scan. Unlinked kits show an unlinked status until you assign them to an individual record.
